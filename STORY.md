# NIGHTFALL — *The Blackthorn Wedding*
### Story bible (the story comes first; every game exists to move it forward)

> `{GROOM}`, `{BRIDE}` and the guests' names and faces are filled in from the host's setup screen and the players' phones.
> Lines in *italics* belong to **Lady Vesper**, the ghost. On the TV they are red, glitching and whispered. Plain lines are the **Narrator**.

---

## The legend

**Blackthorn Manor, October 1925.** Lady Vesper Blackthorn wore ivory silk and her grandmother's ring. Her fiancé, **Edmund Hale**, swore he would be at the altar by midnight.

The chapel bell struck twelve. Edmund never came.

At the reception she smiled and poured the wine herself, a toast "to love that lasts forever". By one o'clock, all ninety-one wedding guests lay dead among the roses and the cake. Vesper climbed into the great hall mirror and was never seen again.

Since then, on the night before any wedding within a hundred miles, the manor **wakes hungry for a groom**. It sends invitations, and it always gets its RSVP.

## PROLOGUE: *The Invitation* (10:55 PM)

**Narrator:**
- "Tonight was supposed to be simple. One last night of freedom for {GROOM}. Good friends, bad decisions, and a sunrise nobody would remember."
- "Then a black envelope arrived. No stamp, no return address, just a wax seal shaped like a broken ring."
- *"You are cordially invited to your last night of freedom."*
- "The car died on the hill road at 10:52. The headlights flickered and went out. When the lightning came, it showed you the house."
- "Blackthorn Manor. The front door was already open."
- "Inside, on a lectern, lies a guest book. Every guest must sign it. **Every guest.**"

**Game: the Guest Book (lobby).** Everyone scans the QR code, writes their name and takes a selfie. Each face appears on the TV as a portrait in the guest book, sepia-toned with candle flicker.

**Game: the Sorting Candles.** Candles light one by one and each guest's portrait drifts to a coven:
- **A: The Ravens** (crimson)
- **B: The Wolves** (ice blue)
- **C: The Serpents** (venom green, used only when there are 8 or more guests)

**Narrator:** "The candles have chosen. From here on, your coven is your only family. Trust them. Mostly."

## CHAPTER I: *The Guest Book* (11:00 PM)

**Narrator:**
- "The doors slam behind you. Every candle in the foyer lights at once."
- "The guest book flips by itself to the last page. Ninety-one names from 1925, in faded ink."
- "Below them, still wet, are your names. Written in blood."
- *"Welcome… my darling guests. You're late. The wedding was supposed to start a hundred years ago."*
- "On the floor, a séance circle has been carved into the wood. Its runes are scattered across your phones. The dead only speak to those who listen together."

**Game: the Séance Circle (team communication puzzle, 2 rounds).**
- Every member of a coven holds a few torn pages of Vesper's diary. Each page describes *one* rune and *its position* in the sigil.
- Only **one** member at a time holds the **ritual candle**. That player is the only one who can tap, and they cannot see the clues for the runes they need.
- The candle passes to the next member after every correct rune, so teammates must *describe* the runes out loud.
- A wrong rune wakes the dead: the sequence resets and there is a jump scare.
- The first coven to complete its sigil wins the round. Its candle stays lit, and every other coven's candle drowns in black wax.

**Narrator (end):**
- "The sigil burns into the floor. Somewhere above you, a music box starts to play."
- "Then every phone in the room buzzes at once. Caller: **{BRIDE}**."
- *"…{GROOM}? {GROOM}, where are you? It's so cold here. There's a woman in my mirror, and she's wearing my face… why am I wearing HER DRESS—"*
- "The line goes dead. Static. Then a woman laughing, very far away."
- "Ring Piece I lies in the ashes of the sigil, a quarter of a silver wedding band. Engraved inside it is a single number."

**Relic for the winning coven: Vesper's Candle.** Their flashlights burn 35% wider in the zombie chapter.

## CHAPTER II: *The Library of Whispers* (11:12 PM)

**Narrator:**
- "The music box led you here: forty thousand books and one lit candle. On the reading desk lies a diary bound in white lace."
- "It is Vesper's. It will not open for strangers. It will only open for someone who truly knows the groom."
- *"Let me see if your friends really love you, {GROOM}. Let me see how well they know you… before I do."*

**Game: the Forbidden Quiz.**
- **Round 1, *The Diary Asks*:** questions about {GROOM}, written by the host in setup. If no host questions were written, these are replaced with "who in this room" votes.
- **Round 2, *Whispers in the Stacks*:** horror and pop-culture trivia.
- **Round 3, *The Accused*:** the diary asks "Who among you…", and everyone votes for a guest's face.
- Answers come from each guest's phone. Faster correct answers score more. A coven's score is the *average* of its members, so team size doesn't matter.

**Narrator (end):**
- "The diary falls open. The last entry is dated October 31st, 1925, 11:59 PM."
- *"He isn't coming. So no one leaves. I poured the wine myself. To love that lasts forever. They looked so peaceful at the tables."*
- "Pressed between the final pages is Ring Piece II, and a note in a different hand: *She keeps her best guests in the reception hall. They're still hungry.*"

**Relic for the winning coven: the Lace Diary.** In the finale they receive one extra true whisper.

## CHAPTER III: *The Nursery* (11:24 PM)

**Narrator:**
- "The lullaby gets louder. The nursery door opens by itself. Inside, a rocking horse is still rocking."
- *"I wanted children, you know. I had names picked out. Let's play a game. Let's see which of you is brave enough to stay."*
- "Vesper will test each of you alone. Every guest plays every trial at once, on their own phone. Every result goes on the wall."

**Game: the Trials of Nerve** (every guest plays individually and simultaneously; the TV shows a live leaderboard and the scares):
1. **Hold the Candle.** Keep your thumb on the flame while Vesper tries to make you flinch. If you lift it, your light dies.
2. **The Music Box.** Repeat her lullaby, one note longer each time. Miss a note and the box snaps shut.
3. **Banish Her.** Tap like your life depends on it. Every coven's taps drive *their* ghost back up the chimney, in a live tug-of-war.
4. **Don't Blink.** Tap only when the **white sigil** appears. Tap on her face and you lose a heartbeat.
5. **The Portrait Gallery.** The TV flashes the guest portraits and some of them are… *wrong*. Count how many were possessed.

Each trial's champion earns a **blessing** for the zombie chapter:

| Trial | Blessing |
|---|---|
| Hold the Candle | Steady Hands (faster fire rate) |
| The Music Box | Lullaby (start with a power) |
| Banish Her | Exorcist (more damage) |
| Don't Blink | Quick Draw (move faster) |
| The Portrait Gallery | Third Eye (longer range) |

**Narrator (end):**
- "The music box stops mid-note. Inside, on a velvet cushion, lies Ring Piece III."
- *"Brave little guests. Come down to dinner. Everyone is waiting."*

**Relic for the winning coven: the Music Box.** Their members start the zombie fight with the **Shotgun**.

## CHAPTER IV: *The Reception Hall* (11:36 PM)

**Narrator:**
- "The reception hall. A banquet set for ninety-one. The cake has collapsed into grey lace and the roses are black."
- "The guests from 1925 are still in their seats, slumped and patient, the way the dead are patient."
- "Someone has poured one more glass of wine. And it seems one of you already drank from it."
- *"One of you belongs to me now. They'll smile at you. They'll help you. And then…"*

**Secret: the Possessed.** One guest's phone turns blood-red: ***You are Possessed.*** Their job is to steer their coven toward the wrong glass without being caught. One of their clue pages is Vesper's lie, and only they know it.

**Game: the Toast (the roast, 2 rounds).**
- *"Every reception needs speeches. Raise a glass to the groom."*
- Everyone answers a prompt about {GROOM} or the night. The host can quietly veto anything before it reaches the TV.
- Answers appear anonymously and everyone votes for their favourite. Each vote earns that player Souls, and the best line is crowned.

**Game: the Poisoned Glass (team logic mystery).**
- Each coven sees six crystal glasses on their phones: different wine, different rims, a lipstick mark here and a crack there.
- Clue pages are split across the coven's members. Together they lead to exactly one poisoned glass.
- The coven's choice locks only when **every member taps the same glass**. A wrong glass means a scare and a 15-second lockout.

**Game: the Unmasking.** Everyone votes on who the Possessed was.
- **Caught:** the possessed guest screams back to normal and every other coven gains Souls.
- **Not caught:** Vesper laughs, and the possessed guest's coven takes the Souls.

**Narrator (end):**
- "Ring Piece IV sits at the bottom of the poisoned glass."
- "The grandfather clock in the corner strikes **eleven forty-five.**"
- "Ninety-one chairs scrape back at once."
- *"Dance with them, darlings. The dead don't dance alone."*
- "**The wedding guests are rising.**"

**Relic for the winning coven: Holy Wine.** Their members get +50% max HP in the zombie chapter.

## CHAPTER V: *The Dead Don't Dance* (11:45 PM)

**Narrator:**
- "Between you and the chapel lies the rose garden and ninety-one dead wedding guests. {BRIDE} is at the altar. Midnight is coming."
- "Your phones are your weapons now. Move with your thumb. Your gun aims itself. Every kill makes you stronger."
- "Get to the chapel. Save {BRIDE}. **Don't let them bite.**"

**Game: the zombie arena** (real-time top-down co-op shooter on the TV, with phones as twin controllers):
- **Wave 1, *The Reception Line*:** walkers in rotting suits and gowns.
- **Wave 2, *The Bridesmaids*:** runners, plus shrieking Bridesmaids whose scream drives the dead into a frenzy.
- **Wave 3, *The Uncles*:** bloated relatives who explode when they get close.
- **Boss, *Edmund, the Groom of the Dead*:** the man who never showed up. He has been waiting a hundred years for his replacement, *and he is wearing {GROOM}'s face.* He charges like a train. When he slams into a wall he's stunned, and his heart glows: that's when to shoot.
- **Weapons** level up with kills: Revolver → Shotgun → Tommy Gun ("the Chicago Typewriter") → Unity Candle (flamethrower) → Wedding Bell (a holy beam that pierces).
- **Powers** dropped by the dead:
  - **Cold Feet:** speed.
  - **Prenup:** shield.
  - **Last Dance:** the dead slow to a waltz.
  - **Best Man:** a ghost ally that fights at your side.
  - **Bouquet Toss:** holy explosion.
- **Holy Water** grenades are thrown with a button.
- Nobody sits out: a fallen guest can be revived by any friend, or rises again on their own after a few seconds.
- Covens compete on kills, revives and boss damage.

**Narrator (end):**
- "Edmund falls to his knees in the roses. For a moment his face is his own again: young, frightened and very, very sorry."
- *"Edmund…?"*
- "He turns to dust. The chapel doors swing open. It is **11:58.**"

## CHAPTER VI: *The Midnight Vow* (11:58 PM)

**Narrator:**
- "The chapel. Candles, roses, and a stained-glass window bleeding moonlight."
- "At the altar stand **three brides**, each wearing a veil and each perfectly still."
- "One of them is {BRIDE}. The other two are Vesper and her reflection."
- "Before you choose, you must reforge the ring. The crypt beneath the altar is sealed with a four-digit lock."

**Game: the Crypt Code (group mystery).**
- Each guest's phone **inventory** holds the clues gathered tonight: "The first digit is odd." "The third digit is the second digit doubled."…
- No single guest can solve it. The whole room must share clues out loud, then anyone can enter the code.
- There are three attempts, and every failure brings midnight closer.

**Game: the Three Brides (final group mystery).**
- Each guest receives a whisper.
- **White whispers** come from {BRIDE}, who is fighting to reach you, and they are true.
- ***Red whispers*** come from Vesper, and every one of them is a lie.
- The TV shows the three brides: veils, bouquets, ring hands, a locket. The room debates, then everyone votes.

**Ending A, *The Vow* (correct bride):**
- "{GROOM} lifts the veil. It's {BRIDE}. Warm, alive, furious, and laughing."
- *"No… NO… he was MINE…"*
- "Vesper shatters like a dropped mirror. Ninety-one sighs rise into the rafters and are gone."
- "The bell strikes midnight, and nothing happens. Nothing at all. It is the most beautiful sound in the world."

**Ending B, *The Eternal Groom* (wrong bride, after the Last Chance):**
- "{GROOM} lifts the veil. Vesper smiles with {BRIDE}'s face."
- *"I do."*
- "…and then {BRIDE} kicks the chapel door off its hinges, because of course she does. Vesper had picked the wrong family to mess with."
- "Dawn comes anyway. It always does."

**Game: the Vow of the Bachelors.** Every guest writes one line of {GROOM}'s wedding vow: *"I, {GROOM}, solemnly swear…"* The host can veto lines. The TV performs the finished vow line by line, with each author's face beside their line.

## EPILOGUE: *Dawn*
- "The sun comes up over Blackthorn Manor. It is just a house again. Old, empty and a little embarrassed."
- "You survived the night. Mostly. The guest book still has your names in it."
- "See you at the wedding."

**Credits** roll every guest's portrait as the "cast". **Awards:**
- **Brain of the Manor:** best quiz and mystery score.
- **First to Die:** first guest downed in the arena.
- **Zombie Slayer:** most kills.
- **Best Toast:** most roast votes.
- **Most Suspicious:** most votes in the Unmasking.
- **Nerves of Steel:** best trial total.
- **MVP:** most Souls overall.

The **winning coven** gets the Blackthorn Trophy.

## Forfeits (*Vesper's Dues*)
- After Chapters I–IV, the guest with the lowest score that chapter draws a forfeit card.
- They can **accept** it, which earns their coven +50 Souls, or **pass** with no penalty. Nobody is ever forced.
- The full lists, mild and spicy (office-safe), live in `server/content/forfeits.js`.

## Content rules
- **Mild:** spooky fun and gentle teasing.
- **Spicy:** sharper roasts and bolder forfeits, but nothing sexual and nothing demeaning about anyone's body, identity or relationships. Every prompt is safe for a mixed office group.
- The host can veto any player-written text before it reaches the TV.
