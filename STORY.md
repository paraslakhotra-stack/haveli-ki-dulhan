# HAVELI KI DULHAN — *a Nightfall story*
### Story bible (the story comes first; every game exists to move it forward)

> `{GROOM}`, `{BRIDE}` and the guests' names and faces are filled in from the host's setup and the phones.
> Lines in *italics* belong to **Rajkumari Chandravati, the Chudail Dulhan**. On the TV they are red, glitching and whispered. Plain lines are the **Sutradhaar** (narrator).
> Tone: Hinglish, spooky and funny, office-safe. No deities or sacred symbols are ever used for horror. The rituals appear respectfully, and in the end the real **saat phere** defeat the ghost.

---

## The legend
**Haveli Kesargarh, Rajasthan, 1925.**
- Rajkumari **Chandravati** sat in the mandap in her red lehenga, mehendi still wet, her payal chiming every time she trembled.
- **Kunwar Vikram Singh**'s baraat was due by midnight. The dhol never came.
- At the hour of vidaai she smiled and served the thandai herself. By dawn, all **108 baraatis** lay still among the marigolds.
- She walked into the sheesh mahal (the hall of mirrors) and never walked out. The villagers say she still walks the haveli in her ghoonghat. Listen for her payal, and never look at her feet: they point **backwards**.
- Every night before a wedding, the haveli hungers for a dulha.

## PROLOGUE: *Shaadi Ka Card* (10:55 PM)
- "Tonight was supposed to be simple. {GROOM}'s last night of azaadi (freedom). Dost (friends), dhamaal (chaos), and a sunrise nobody would remember."
- "Then a wedding card arrived. Blood-red. No sender. Sealed with sindoor in the shape of a broken anguthi (ring)."
- *"Aapko saadar aamantrit kiya jaata hai… aapki azaadi ki aakhri raat par."* ("You are cordially invited… to the last night of your freedom.")
- "The car died on the desert highway at 10:52. When the lightning came, it showed you the haveli."
- "Haveli Kesargarh. Its gates were already open."
- **Lobby game:** sign the **haveli ka register** (the haveli's guest register) with a name and a selfie.
- **Sorting game:** the **diyas** choose your teams:
  - **Team Naag** (cobras, venom green)
  - **Team Ullu** (owls, moon blue)
  - **Team Kaali Billi** (black cats, crimson; only with 8 or more guests)

## CHAPTER I: *Haveli Ka Darwaza* (11:00 PM)
- "Behind you, the giant doors slam shut. Every diya in the courtyard lights itself."
- "The register flips to its last page. The names of 108 baraatis from 1925 in faded ink. And below them… your names. Still wet. In sindoor."
- *"Aa gaye baraati? Sau saal late ho."* ("So the baraatis have arrived? You're a hundred years late.")
- "A rangoli is drawn on the courtyard floor, but its symbols are scattered across your phones."
- **Game: Tantrik Rangoli.** The clue pages are torn **janam-patri** (horoscope) pages.
  - Only the one holding the **diya** can tap a symbol, and the diya passes on after every correct symbol.
  - So the team has to *describe* the symbols out loud.
  - A wrong symbol wakes the dead.
- **End of chapter:**
  - Every phone gets a video call from {BRIDE}: *"{GROOM}? Kahaan ho? Yahan bahut thand hai… mere haath ki mehendi kisi aur ke naam ki hai… yeh lehenga mera nahi hai—"* ("Where are you? It's so cold here… the mehendi on my hands carries someone else's name… this lehenga isn't mine—")
  - Static. A woman's laughter, and payal.
  - **Ring piece I:** a quarter of a silver sagai ki anguthi (engagement ring), with something engraved inside.

## CHAPTER II: *Pothi Khana* (11:12 PM)
- "The payal led you here: shelves of cloth-bound pothis (handwritten manuscripts) and one burning diya. On the desk lies a diary wrapped in red silk."
- *"Dekhte hain tumhare dost tumhe kitna jaante hain, {GROOM}… mujhse pehle."* ("Let's see how well your friends know you, {GROOM}… before I do.")
- **Game: Forbidden Quiz.**
  - **"Diary Poochti Hai"** ("the diary asks"): questions about {GROOM}, written by the host.
  - **Shaadi trivia and Bollywood horror.**
  - **"Kaun hai jo…?"** ("who is the one who…?"): votes on each other's faces.
- **End of chapter:** the last diary entry, 31 October 1925, 11:59 PM: *"Woh nahi aaya. Toh koi nahi jaayega. Thandai maine khud parosi."* ("He didn't come. So no one leaves. I served the thandai myself.")
  - A note in another hand: *"Uske baraati abhi bhi daawat mein baithe hain. Bhookhe."* ("Her baraatis are still sitting at the feast. Hungry.")
  - **Ring piece II.**

## CHAPTER III: *Zenana Ka Jhoola* (11:24 PM)
- "In the zenana (the women's quarters), a jhoola (swing) is swinging. Nobody is on it. Somewhere, someone is humming a lori (lullaby)."
- *"Mere bhi sapne the. Chalo, ek khel khelte hain. Dekhte hain kaun sabse bahadur hai."* ("I had dreams too. Come, let's play a game. Let's see who is the bravest.")
- **Game: Trials of Nerve.** Everyone plays alone, all at once, on their own phone:
  1. **Diya Pakdo:** keep your thumb on the diya flame while she tries to make you flinch.
  2. **Payal Ki Taal:** repeat her ghungroo rhythm, one beat longer each time.
  3. **Nimbu-Mirchi Bhagao:** tap furiously to push her back up the chimney. It's a tug-of-war between teams.
  4. **Pairon Ko Mat Dekho:** tap only on the **nazar charm**. Tap on her backward feet and you lose.
  5. **Shaadi Album:** the TV flashes the guests' photos, and some of them are *possessed*. Count them.
- The champion of each trial earns a **blessing** for the zombie baraat: Steady Haath, Lori, Ojha, Cheetah, Teesri Aankh.
- **Ring piece III.**

## CHAPTER IV: *Sangeet Ki Daawat* (11:36 PM)
- "The shamiana (wedding tent) still stands. Fairy lights flicker. The 108 baraatis sit at the pattals (leaf plates)… still. Patient."
- "Someone has poured one more glass of thandai. And one of you… has already drunk it."
- **Secret role:** one guest is **Possessed**. Their job is to steer their team to the wrong glass, and one of their clue pages is Chandravati's lie.
- **Game: Sangeet Roast.** Hinglish prompts about {GROOM}. The host can veto anything before it reaches the TV, then everyone votes.
- **Game: Zehreeli Thandai** (poisoned thandai).
  - Six glasses: rose sharbat, thandai or kesar doodh (saffron milk); silver or gold rims; a lipstick mark or a crack.
  - The clues are split across the team. The glass only locks when **every member picks the same one**.
- **Game: the Unmasking.** Everyone votes on who was possessed.
- **End of chapter:** the clock strikes **11:45**. 108 chairs scrape back at once.
  - *"Nacho, baraatiyon. Murde akele nahi naachte."* ("Dance, baraatis. The dead never dance alone.")
  - **The murda baraat rises.** **Ring piece IV.**

## CHAPTER V: *Solah Shringar Ki Khoj* (11:41 PM) — the split-screen treasure hunt
- The dhol grows louder in the baagh; the dead are still climbing out of the earth. Then {BRIDE}'s voice comes from every mirror at once: Chandravati has scattered her **solah shringar** (the sixteen bridal adornments) through the haveli. Without them, no one in the mandap will know which dulhan is real.
- **Game:** every team gets its own trail of **5 stations** through the haveli (Pothi Khana, Zenana, Sheesh Mahal, Rasoi, Diwan-e-Khaas, Tehkhana, Baoli). The TV **splits into one pane per team** (2, 3 or 4), each with its own camera and a **mini-map** whose glowing hint zone shrinks towards the next station.
  - Phones show the **riddle** ("Jahan kabhi rotiyan sikti thi…") and a **garam/thanda** meter.
  - Reach the glowing sandook and a **shaadi question** appears on the team's phones. Right answer → an adornment (chooda, kaleere, maang tikka, nath, payal, gajra…) and the next riddle. Wrong → the team is locked out for 8 seconds and Chandravati comes looking.
  - Two of **Chandravati's shadows** drift through the halls. A touch freezes you for 3 seconds; **raise your diya** to push her back.
  - Trailing teams get sharper hints (catch-up), and the hunt ends after 8 minutes or when every team is done.
- **Every adornment becomes power in the baagh:** 1 = +15% HP, 2 = a better starting gun, 3 = a free power, 4+ = a faster dash.
- **End:** "In the baagh, the dhol stops. A hundred and eight heads turn towards the haveli."

## CHAPTER VI: *Murda Baraat* (11:47 PM)
- "Between you and the mandap lies the haveli's baagh (garden), and 108 dead baraatis dancing to a dhol nobody is playing."
- "Your phones are your weapons." Every phone becomes a **twin-stick gamepad**: left thumb moves, right thumb aims, and the guns **never stop firing** (let go of aim and they lock onto the nearest zombie). 💨 Dash dodges through the horde; 🍋 bombs go where you aim.
- **Waves:**
  1. **The Baraat Arrives:** dead baraatis.
  2. **The Gossip Aunties:** shrieking aunties who send the dead into a frenzy, plus fast **naagin dancers**.
  3. **Fufaji Is Angry:** bloated, offended Fufajis who **explode** when they reach you.
  4. **Boss: Kunwar Vikram, the Bhoot Dulha** (ghost groom). Sherwani and safa (turban), charging like a runaway ghodi (wedding horse), and wearing **{GROOM}'s face**. When he slams into a wall he is stunned and his heart glows. Shoot it!
- **Weapons** level up with kills: Desi Katta → Dunali (double-barrel) → Tommy Gun → Phuljhadi Flamer (sparkler flamethrower) → Anaar Rocket.
- **Powers:**
  - **Bhaag Milkha:** speed.
  - **Nazar Battu:** a shield.
  - **Naagin Dance:** the dead slow down to dance.
  - **Bestie Bhai:** a ghost ally.
  - **Gulaal Bomb:** a colour explosion.
- **Nimbu-Mirchi bombs** are thrown with a button.
- Any friend can revive a fallen guest, and nobody sits out.
- **Baraat Break:** between waves 2 and 3 the fight pauses for one shaadi question on every phone. Get it right and a power drops into your hands.
- **End of chapter:** Vikram falls to his knees. For a moment his face is his own again, young and afraid. *"Maaf karna, Chandra…"* ("Forgive me, Chandra…") He turns to dust. It is 11:58.

## CHAPTER VII: *Aadhi Raat Ka Mandap* (11:58 PM)
- "The mandap: marigolds, banana leaves, the havan kund (sacred fire pit) glowing softly. **Three dulhans** sit in red, all in ghoonghat, all perfectly still."
- "One of them is {BRIDE}. The other two are Chandravati and her reflection."
- **Game: Tijori Ka Taala** (the vault lock). The engravings collected all night are spread across the phones. Share them out loud, open the 4-digit vault, and make the ring whole.
- **Phere Quiz:** before the real pheras, seven rapid shaadi questions, one for every phera. The team that knows shaadi best hears **one more true whisper** in the next game.
- **Game: Teen Dulhanein** (three brides).
  - **White whispers** come from {BRIDE} and are true. **Red whispers** come from Chandravati, and every one is a lie.
  - The dulhans differ in their ghoonghat (long, short or torn), varmala (genda, gulab or mogra), chooda (red or white bangles) and nath (nose ring).
- **Win:** {GROOM} lifts the ghoonghat. It's {BRIDE}, angry, alive and laughing. The real saat phere begin. With each phera, Chandravati fades, until she shatters like a mirror and 108 souls rise into the sky.
- **Lose (after one last chance):** Chandravati smiles from under the ghoonghat: *"Qubool hai… oh wait, galat shaadi."* ("I accept… oh wait, wrong wedding.") Then {BRIDE} kicks the mandap's door down, because that's {BRIDE}.
- **Game: Saat Vachan of the Bachelors.** Every guest writes one of {GROOM}'s wedding vows, and the TV reads them out as "Pehla vachan… Doosra vachan…" ("the first vow… the second vow…").

## EPILOGUE: *Vidaai at Sunrise*
- "The sun rises over Kesargarh. It's just an old haveli again. A shehnai plays somewhere far away."
- "You survived the night. The register still holds your names. See you at the shaadi!"
- **Credits** show everyone's photos. **Awards:** Brain of the Haveli, Pehla Shikaar (first to fall), Zombie Slayer, Best Roast, Sabse Shady (most suspicious), Nerves of Steel, MVP. The winning team gets the **Kesargarh Trophy**.
