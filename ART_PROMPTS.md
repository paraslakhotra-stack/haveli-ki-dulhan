# Haveli Ki Dulhan — character & key-art prompts (Google Flow)

The game already ships with interim art and falls back to its own painted scenes, so every image here is optional.
Make them in **Google Flow** (labs.google/flow), then upload them to a Google Drive folder called **"Haveli Art"**.
Name each file as listed (any format/size; the game converts them to WebP at 1600 px for the TV and 720 px for phones).

**Style line — paste at the end of every prompt so the set matches:**
> painterly dark Rajasthani gothic, cinematic candle-light and moonlight, desaturated palette with deep crimson and antique gold accents, film grain, 16:9, no text, no logos, eerie but not gory

Keep it office-safe: no blood, no gore, no deities or sacred symbols.
