Midnight strikes… and nothing happens. Kuch bhi nahi. The most beautiful sound in the world.

The dulha lifts the ghoonghat. Chandravati smiles back with the dulhan's face.

…and then the real dulhan kicks the mandap ka darwaza off its hinges. Because that is our dulhan. Chandravati picked the wrong family to mess with.

Sunrise comes anyway. It always does.

One last rasam. A shaadi needs vachan. Every guest writes one of the dulha's wedding vows — the Saat Vachan of the Bachelors.

The sun rises over Kesargarh. It is just an old haveli again. Somewhere far away, a shehnai plays.

You survived the night. Mostly. The register still holds your names.

Milte hain shaadi mein!
