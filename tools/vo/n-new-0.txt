Outside, the dhol grows louder. The dead are still climbing out of the baagh. There is a little time.

Then the dulhan's voice comes from every mirror at once. Chandravati has scattered her solah shringar through the haveli. Without it, no one will know which dulhan is real.

Split up. Every team has its own trail. Your phones hold the riddle; the map on screen shows where to look — and it sharpens with time.

Reach the glowing spot and a shaadi question appears on your phones. Answer right, and the next riddle unlocks. Answer wrong… and she comes looking for you.

If a shadow drifts towards you, raise your diya. Whatever you find now will fight beside you in the baagh.

The searching is over. Whatever the haveli gave back, you carry with you now.

In the baagh, the dhol stops. A hundred and eight heads turn towards the haveli.

Before the real pheras, seven questions — one for every phera. The team that knows shaadi best will hear one more true whisper.
